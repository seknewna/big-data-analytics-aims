# Further reading

## Books

- Kleppmann, M. *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*. O'Reilly. — The best conceptual foundation for distributed data systems.
- Chambers, B. & Zaharia, M. *Spark: The Definitive Guide*. O'Reilly.
- Damji, J. et al. *Learning Spark* (2nd ed.). O'Reilly. — Free PDF available from Databricks.
- Leskovec, J., Rajaraman, A. & Ullman, J. *Mining of Massive Datasets*. Cambridge University Press. Free at [mmds.org](http://www.mmds.org/), with lecture slides and videos.
- Reis, J. & Housley, M. *Fundamentals of Data Engineering*. O'Reilly.
- Akidau, T., Chernyak, S. & Lax, R. *Streaming Systems*. O'Reilly.
- Rey, S., Arribas-Bel, D. & Wolf, L. *Geographic Data Science with Python*. Free online.
- Dwork, C. & Roth, A. *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*. Free online.
- Barocas, S., Hardt, M. & Narayanan, A. *Fairness and Machine Learning*. Free at fairmlbook.org.

## Foundational papers

- Ghemawat, Gobioff & Leung (2003). *The Google File System*. SOSP.
- Dean & Ghemawat (2004). *MapReduce: Simplified Data Processing on Large Clusters*. OSDI.
- Zaharia et al. (2012). *Resilient Distributed Datasets*. NSDI.
- Armbrust et al. (2015). *Spark SQL: Relational Data Processing in Spark*. SIGMOD.
- Zaharia et al. (2016). *Apache Spark: A Unified Engine for Big Data Processing*. Communications of the ACM.
- Akidau et al. (2015). *The Dataflow Model*. VLDB.
- McSherry, Isard & Murray (2015). *Scalability! But at what COST?* HotOS.
- Armbrust et al. (2021). *Lakehouse*. CIDR.
- Raasveldt & Mühleisen (2019). *DuckDB: an Embeddable Analytical Database*. SIGMOD.
- Flajolet et al. (2007). *HyperLogLog*. AofA.

## Big data for development and Africa

- Blumenstock, Cadamuro & On (2015). *Predicting poverty and wealth from mobile phone metadata*. Science.
- Jean et al. (2016). *Combining satellite imagery and machine learning to predict poverty*. Science.
- Yeh et al. (2020). *Using publicly available satellite imagery and deep learning to understand economic well-being in Africa*. Nature Communications.
- Nekoto et al. (2020). *Participatory Research for Low-resourced Machine Translation: A Case Study in African Languages*. Findings of EMNLP (Masakhane).
- Taylor, L. (2017). *What is data justice? The case for connecting digital rights and freedoms globally*. Big Data & Society.
